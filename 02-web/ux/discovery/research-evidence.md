Account Owner and B2B Buyer, with eight recorded profiles. Evidence is
qualitative and historical. Repeated frictions include fragmented tools,
manual validation, stock mismatch, weak delivery evidence and slow UX.

- `REFINE`: problem framing, segment language and validation hypotheses against
