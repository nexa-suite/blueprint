# Tokens

Hierarchy: primitive values, semantic roles, component tokens and
data-visualization tokens. Consumers use semantic roles; raw primitives do not
encode business meaning or surface ownership.

Maturity: `Stable` means reviewed and consumed; `Candidate` means proposed for
adoption; `Experimental` means lab-only or under evaluation.

Design Lab remains executable reference. Blueprint records policy and links; it
does not duplicate token implementation.
