# September 2026 Owner decisions

**Decision status:** `OWNER-ACCEPTED` / `ACCEPTED` where stated below.

**Provenance:** explicit Owner decisions in the September 2026 canonical
knowledge reconciliation mission. This record is a Product and governance
decision source; it is not implementation evidence, research evidence or
Product Acceptance evidence.

## Business and market direction — OWNER-ACCEPTED

- Nexa is **Peru-first** for its present commercial and Product direction.
- International and broader LATAM expansion is a distant **FUTURE** direction,
  not a current V1 commitment.
- Future expansion must not be bound mechanically to the existing Mobile V4 /
  Future backlog nomenclature. A later Owner decision is required before that
  backlog is used for international scope.
- Cold-chain remains a strong specialization and differentiator. It is not the
  total market definition and does not create a Cold Chain Bounded Context.

This direction does not change the accepted eleven Bounded Contexts, the
Tenant 1:1 Workspace relationship or the V1 Product closure.

## Research history — ACCEPTED provenance rule

- Historical Web research interviews were performed in **2026**. They are
  historical research evidence, not 2025 evidence.
- Historical findings may seed current hypotheses and research questions, but
  they do not automatically become current Product requirements.
- Legacy Web architecture, domain semantics, role terminology, ordering
  terminology and subscription assumptions remain historical or superseded
  unless a current Owner decision explicitly promotes them.

## Mobile direction — OWNER-ACCEPTED for planning

- Sales remains outside prioritized Mobile V1. Sales convenience capabilities
  may be reconsidered in a later release.
- Buyer Mobile may preserve a human escalation or contact route for delivery
  exceptions. This does not authorize or require an in-app chat system, direct
  Driver contact or a new communication capability.
- Offline and connectivity needfinding is actively being researched. Current
  online-first and server-authoritative rules remain in force.
- Scanning is justified only when task and research evidence demonstrates
  usefulness. It is not a universal Mobile requirement; manual alternatives
  remain necessary where the task permits them.
- Competitor evidence must be periodically revalidated before it is used to
  support a Product decision.

The 73 canonical Mobile functional stories remain distinct from the 97-item
academic backlog. These decisions do not promote personas, hypotheses, Spikes,
Technical Stories or planned experiments into accepted Product truth.

## Knowledge promotion — ACCEPTED governance

- The Owner is the final authority for promoting unresolved Product or Business
  knowledge into accepted canon.
- Imported knowledge must be classified before promotion. Useful content may
  remain `PROPOSED`, `RESEARCHING`, `OPEN`, `HISTORICAL_ONLY` or
  `SUPERSEDED`.
- Use the existing canonical taxonomy. `OWNER-ACCEPTED` / `ACCEPTED` means
  approved semantics; `IMPLEMENTED` means repository evidence;
  `TECHNICALLY VERIFIED` means named checks; `PRODUCT ACCEPTED` means Product
  acceptance evidence; `SUPERSEDED` / `RETIRED` preserves historical closure.
- Do not introduce a generic ambiguous `VALIDATED` state.
- Mobile Report is the most complete current academic UML projection. Where it
  adds useful semantics, reconcile those semantics into Blueprint and keep the
  report as a derived academic projection rather than a competing authority.
- Public reports may contain canonical Nexa semantics, but they must not
  advertise Blueprint as a public facilitator, dependency or selling point.
- Future GPT/Nexa memory projections must be generated from clear canonical
  files and must reverify live refs and releases before regeneration. The final
  memory pack is not generated by this reconciliation.

## Website boundary — OPEN / OUT OF SCOPE for this wave

Website commercial pricing, plans and registration drift are explicitly out of
scope for this reconciliation. Any such issue remains an open source-side
question and is not promoted, redesigned or resolved here.
