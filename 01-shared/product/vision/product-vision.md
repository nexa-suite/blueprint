# Product vision

Nexa is a fast, role-focused B2B multi-tenant SaaS platform for importers, distributors and wholesalers, particularly cold-chain businesses, designed to coordinate commercial operations, inventory, fulfillment and delivery while providing buyers with a simple and reliable purchasing experience.

Nexa is positioned as an ERP/CRM-oriented operational platform for B2B importers, distributors and wholesalers. Cold-chain capability is a strong specialization, not a restriction that excludes other suitable tenants. The accepted product direction is consolidated in the [Product & Business V1 baseline](../product-baseline.md).

Current product/architecture differentiators:

- Strong Tenant isolation
- Role-focused simplicity
- High perceived responsiveness
- Operational traceability
- B2B buyer self-service
- Cold-chain awareness

Measurable quality scenarios will be defined later.
