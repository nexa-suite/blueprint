# ICP and market hypotheses

**Status:** `PROPOSED` / `RESEARCHING`.

These are reference hypotheses for discovery and business modelling. They are
not market evidence, a universal persona definition or Owner-accepted Product
scope.

## Reference ICP Hypothesis v0.1 — PROPOSED

Candidate fit:

- Peru-first market direction.
- B2B importer, distributor or wholesaler.
- Initially small-to-medium operational complexity.
- Candidate band: **5–30 internal users**.
- Candidate band: **1–3 warehouses or locations**.
- Candidate band: **200–3,000 SKUs**.
- Candidate band: **100–2,000 orders per month**.
- Lots, expiry and cold-chain handling strengthen fit but are not mandatory.
- Minimum fit is sufficient commercial, warehouse and delivery coordination
  complexity for fragmented context to become costly.

Every numeric band is a hypothesis, not observed market evidence. The bands
must be tested against current discovery and periodically revalidated.

International/LATAM expansion is `FUTURE` and is not inferred from the Mobile
V4/Future backlog.

## Economic buyer hypothesis — PROPOSED

The buying group may vary by company size and maturity:

| Role | Candidate contribution | Evidence state |
|---|---|---|
| Owner / General Manager | Economic buyer and signer in a small company | PROPOSED |
| General Management / Operations | Economic buyer in a larger company | PROPOSED |
| Business Operations Manager | Likely champion | PROPOSED |
| Operations users | Workflow-fit evaluators and daily adopters | PROPOSED |
| IT / Security | Technical-risk evaluator when present | PROPOSED |
| Finance / Administration | Commercial-viability evaluator | PROPOSED |

This is a buying-group hypothesis, not a frozen universal persona.

## Research needed — RESEARCHING

Test segment fit, operational complexity, current coordination cost, buyer
authority, deployment constraints, incumbent ERP/accounting relationships and
the problem severity that makes Nexa worth adopting. Historical 2026 Web
research can seed questions but cannot close these hypotheses by itself.
