# Business case and TCO

**Status:** `PROPOSED` / `RESEARCHING`.

Business cases must keep distinct concepts separate. They must not collapse
them into one number:

1. Software or project implementation budget.
2. Customer Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).
3. Cost-Benefit.
4. Return on Investment (ROI).
5. Payback Period.
6. Net Present Value (NPV / VAN).
7. Sensitivity analysis.
8. Nexa SaaS unit economics.

## Proposed customer TCO convention

Use a **3-year analytical horizon** by default for a proposed customer TCO
scenario. The horizon is a modelling convention, not an observed customer
commitment. State implementation, subscription, internal change, migration,
integration, training, support and avoided-cost assumptions separately.

## Simulated economic evidence

Academic or business modelling may use a reference scenario only when every
number is labeled **SIMULATED / ASSUMPTION**. Simulated numbers must never be
described as interview findings, observed company data, Product Acceptance or
market evidence.

No monetary inputs are supplied by this canonical area. Actual inputs remain
`OPEN` until sourced and reviewed.
