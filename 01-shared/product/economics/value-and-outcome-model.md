# Value and outcome model

**Status:** `PROPOSED` / `RESEARCHING`.

## Proposed primary operational/business metric

### Commercial Commitment Fulfillment Reliability

Candidate definition:

> The proportion of confirmed Commercial Commitments fulfilled and delivered
> within the agreed operational obligation, without an unresolved
> inventory, preparation, delivery or Buyer-receipt exception attributable to
> coordination failure.

The denominator, time window, treatment of partial delivery, approved
exceptions, cancellation and data-quality exclusions remain `OPEN`. No measured
result is claimed.

## Candidate secondary measures — PROPOSED

- Inventory-related exception rate.
- Order coordination time.
- Manual reconciliation hours.
- Picking or discrepancy rate.
- Expiry/waste exposure.
- Delivery discrepancy rate.
- Buyer exception-resolution time.

Measures require operational definitions, data availability, baseline periods
and research consent before use as evidence. A green technical check cannot be
reported as an outcome improvement.
