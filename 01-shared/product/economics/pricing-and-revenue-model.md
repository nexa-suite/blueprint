# Pricing and revenue model

**Status:** `RESEARCHING`.

This is a proposed pricing direction. No public monetary price or plan matrix
is canonized.

## Proposed principles

- Base subscription per Tenant.
- Scale with broad operational bands such as active internal users and
  warehouses or locations.
- Evaluate transaction or order bands later, only if evidence supports them.
- Do not primarily penalize adoption through strict per-seat pricing unless
  research demonstrates that it is appropriate.
- Onboarding, exceptional migration, custom integrations and consulting may be
  Professional Services.
- Security, tenant isolation, authorization, historical integrity and core
  correctness must never be premium feature gates.
- Do not publish or promote a monetary amount from this document.

## Open commercial questions

Packaging, contract term, billing unit, migration policy, integration policy,
support boundary, discounting, tax treatment and willingness-to-pay evidence
remain `OPEN`.

Website pricing/plans/registration drift is outside this reconciliation wave;
this model does not repair or override Website Product decisions.
