# Business economics

This area contains proposed and researching Business/Economic knowledge. It is
not Owner-accepted market truth, measured company evidence, Product Acceptance
or a public pricing commitment.

## Status boundary

- `PROPOSED`: planning model or hypothesis that still requires evidence and
  Owner promotion.
- `RESEARCHING`: active evidence work; not a frozen policy or result.
- `OPEN`: unresolved decision or missing input.
- `SIMULATED / ASSUMPTION`: academic or business-model scenario only; never an
  interview finding or observed company result.

Security, tenant isolation, authorization, historical integrity and core
correctness are Product foundations. They are not premium feature gates.
