# Buying process

**Status:** `PROPOSED` / `RESEARCHING`.

The buying process is a commercial hypothesis, not an implemented Nexa
workflow and not a universal customer process.

## Candidate buying motion

1. Contact or referral.
2. Qualification.
3. Discovery.
4. Demonstration.
5. Agreement.
6. Assisted onboarding.
7. Configuration.
8. Controlled data import.
9. Role-based training.
10. Controlled go-live.
11. 30/60/90-day review.

The exact signer, champion, approval path, migration responsibility, contract
form and integration boundary remain `RESEARCHING` / `OPEN`.

## Buying-group boundary

Use the [economic buyer hypothesis](icp-and-market-hypotheses.md) as a
question set, not as a fixed persona model. Nexa Commercial & Onboarding Staff
may support evaluation and provisioning, but this document does not authorize
new Website, Platform or Mobile implementation behavior.
