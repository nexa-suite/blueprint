# Design baseline

Nexa needs a calm, role-focused B2B experience for importers and distributors. The Internal Web Platform serves workforce operations; the Buyer Portal serves self-service purchasing and delivery visibility; the Public Website serves product discovery and contact/demo. These surfaces remain distinct.

Current baseline: preserve existing Nexa product direction and evidence. Do not infer a new visual system from legacy screens, application folders or framework defaults. Do not claim visual parity without matched authenticated captures and an evidence matrix.

Deferred: final brand tokens, component inventory, responsive specifications, buyer/workforce information architecture closure and production accessibility sign-off.
