# Color

Use primitive ramps only through semantic roles. The baseline keeps Nexa blue/slate as its identity and provides documented semantic families for success, warning/attention, danger, operational orange, refrigerated sky and frozen indigo.

Semantic status colors describe meaning and require text/icon/shape support. Categorical and quantitative visualization palettes are separate roles; danger red is not an arbitrary chart series. Pure white product surfaces remain canonical where product identity and media need neutral presentation.

Every new color needs a role, contrast evidence, usage boundary and token source. Raw colors in reusable UI are prohibited.
