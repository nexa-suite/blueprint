# Shape and radius

Geometry is semantic, not a universal rounded-card treatment. Controls, cards, panels, overlays, navigation rows, status chips and circular markers use distinct approved roles. Product surfaces avoid hard square corners while preserving purposeful variation.

Selected, hover, pressed, focus and loading layers inherit the base geometry. Internal layers and pseudo-elements clip or inherit where needed. The approved Sidebar geometry is not reopened by generic radius normalization.
