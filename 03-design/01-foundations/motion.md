# Motion

Standard motion communicates a meaningful state change in a short interval. Reduced Motion removes spatial/rotational dependence while retaining labels, focus, ordering and terminal meaning. Compare both modes for button feedback, overlays/disclosure, progress/activity and dispatch movement.

Indeterminate activity must not rely on rotating text. Temporal evidence stops at terminal states and requires explicit recovery for retryable errors.
