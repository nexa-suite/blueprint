# Brand

The canonical Nexa logo is the supplied brand asset and remains unchanged across the light baseline. Do not redraw the wordmark in text or CSS. The brand plane may create a restrained entry moment for Authentication, but it must not turn every product surface into a marketing panel.

Nexa blue/slate identity, Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter hierarchy and mono utility text are the accepted candidate foundation. Dark mode, liquid glass and unrelated visual identities are deferred.
