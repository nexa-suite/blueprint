# Contribution and review

Every change names the affected surface, owner, maturity and evidence source. Reviewers check semantic purpose before visual polish.

## Required review questions

- Does the change preserve the frozen Nexa visual foundation?
- Is the behavior reusable, documentation-only or Lab-only?
- Are tokens, geometry, content and non-color cues documented?
- Are default, focus, hover, pressed, loading, disabled, error and recovery states covered where relevant?
- Does the composition preserve Tenant, role, status and product vocabulary?
- What deterministic and human evidence remains open?

The Design Lab may implement a candidate specimen without promoting it to a production contract. Adoption requires a recorded decision and a public API review.
