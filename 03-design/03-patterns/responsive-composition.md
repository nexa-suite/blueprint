# Responsive composition

Evaluate 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 320 widths. Protect task hierarchy, readable measure, control targets and local data overflow. At 200% text and 400% reflow, content must remain understandable without a duplicated mobile product or negative-margin hacks.
