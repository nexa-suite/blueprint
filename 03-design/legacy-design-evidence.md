# Legacy design evidence

Legacy Vue screens, assets and academic material are historical evidence only. They may inform later discovery of workflows, terminology and usability risks after provenance and participant rights are reviewed. They do not define current colors, tokens, components, information architecture, accessibility or TARGET product behavior.

The [legacy ecosystem report](../11-reference/legacy-ecosystem-report/README.md) is the provenance index. Binary assets remain local unless explicitly cleared.
