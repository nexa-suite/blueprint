# Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA as a quality goal, with current product context and human review. Evidence covers semantic structure, labels, error association, keyboard access, visible focus, target geometry, resize/reflow, reduced motion, increased contrast and non-color-only status. A Lab page must not claim certification.

Assistive-technology validation remains a human handoff and is not inferred from automated tests or screenshots.
