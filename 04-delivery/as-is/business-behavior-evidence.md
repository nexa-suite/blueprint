## Evidence limits

Runtime health proves local process/container availability, not production provider readiness. Unit and integration success proves the checked scenarios, not complete product closure. Platform and Portal authenticated browser behavior remains a `BLOQUEO` until approved local credentials/fixtures are supplied without exposing secrets.
