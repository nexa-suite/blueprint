# Surfaces and elevation

Light canonical appearance is the v1 baseline: cool page canvas, white structural/product surfaces, quiet inset planes and restrained borders. Elevation is purposeful for menus, dialogs and transient overlays; do not add a drop shadow to every element.

Product imagery and product cards use a white presentation surface unless the product contract explicitly requires another treatment. Auth brand atmosphere is a bounded composition exception.
