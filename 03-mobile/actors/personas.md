# Mobile research-informed synthetic personas

These personas are research-informed synthetic artifacts derived from the
final AV1 Needfinding evidence. They are **NOT INTERVIEW PARTICIPANTS** and do
not constitute Product Acceptance or solution validation.

## Alejandro Vargas

- **Segment:** MOB-SEG-01 — Warehouse & Dispatch Operations.
- **Represents:** physical preparation and verification, exception resolution
  and continuity into dispatch.
- **Boundary:** Warehouse Operator and Dispatch Coordinator responsibilities
  remain related but distinct; this persona does not merge the roles.

## Diego Morales

- **Segment:** MOB-SEG-02 — Driver Delivery Execution.
- **Represents:** assigned delivery, movement, incidents, mobile constraints,
  evidence and outcome explanation.
- **Boundary:** Driver outcome is not Buyer receipt.

## Carlos Mendoza

- **Segment:** MOB-SEG-03 — B2B Buyers.
- **Represents:** supply continuity, provider coordination, delivery follow-up,
  exception handling and receipt when applicable.
- **Boundary:** detailed physical receipt is not universal across the sample.
