# Mobile segments

The current Mobile research segments are baselined from final AV1 Needfinding
evidence. Segments are not roles, permissions, Bounded Contexts, applications
or deployment units. Product Generation and story lifecycle remain separate
from this research classification.

| ID | Segment | Primary actors | Research state |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOB-SEG-01 | Warehouse & Dispatch Operations | Warehouse Operator; Dispatch Coordinator | Research evidence available; solution validation open. |
| MOB-SEG-02 | Driver Delivery Execution | Driver / Delivery Operator | Research evidence available; solution validation open. |
| MOB-SEG-03 | B2B Buyers | Customer Buyer | Research evidence available; solution validation open. |

Sales Representative remains a shared Nexa actor and is not a prioritized
current Mobile research segment. Nexa Platform remains the principal complete
commercial surface; Mobile commercial convenience remains a later candidate in
the course-end Mobile V1 planning envelope.

See [Mobile actors](../actors/README.md) and [segment boundaries](proposed-segments.md).
