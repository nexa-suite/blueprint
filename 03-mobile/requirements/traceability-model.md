# Mobile requirements traceability model

Future mapping:

`MOB-US -> Mobile app -> actor/segment -> shared capability -> primary/supporting
BC -> process/state/event -> research evidence -> Design projection -> technical
spike -> implementation/validation evidence`.

Reserve namespaces `MOB-EPIC-*`, `MOB-US-*`, `MOB-TS-*` and `MOB-SPIKE-*` for
epics, validated stories, technical work and research/technical spikes.
Do not generate IDs now. A Mobile story cannot be marked validated from Web
implementation or historical academic wording alone.

Research status must remain `PENDING` until participant, method, date,
provenance, observed need, confidence and interpretation are recorded. Product
authority remains [Shared Product](../../01-shared/product/README.md); Mobile
does not create BCs or change accepted business semantics.
