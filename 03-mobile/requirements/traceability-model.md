# Mobile requirements traceability model

Canonical chain:

`MOB-US → App → Actor/Segment → CAP-01..CAP-16 → primary/secondary BC →
process/state → AC → research evidence → Design projection → TS/SPIKE →
implementation → technical/Product/System Acceptance`.

The [V1 catalog](mobile-v1-catalog.md) owns story behavior and AC. The
[reconciliation](reconciliation.md) owns historical continuity. The shared
technical catalog owns TS-001..020; no duplicate MOB-TS namespace is created.

Research status stays `PROPOSED / RESEARCH VALIDATION PENDING` until participant,
method, date, provenance, observed need, confidence and interpretation are
recorded. Backend evidence never becomes Mobile client evidence. Product and
BC authority remain in [Shared Product](../../01-shared/product/README.md) and
[Strategic DDD](../../01-shared/domain/strategic-ddd/README.md).
