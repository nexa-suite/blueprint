# Mobile V1 Story-to-Design Readiness

This is the single readiness map for the 28 Mobile V1 stories. It gives
Design and research enough context to explore flows without deciding screens,
visual treatment or framework prematurely. The canonical behavior remains in
[mobile-v1-catalog.md](mobile-v1-catalog.md); this document does not create
new Product rules, Bounded Contexts or client implementation evidence.

Terms:

- **Stale:** a previously read fact whose freshness or authorization is no
  longer sufficient for the next action.
- **Connectivity:** V1 is online-first. A draft, safe read cache or temporary
  evidence can survive a brief interruption, but an authoritative mutation
  needs confirmation.
- **Device:** device capabilities are inputs and evidence aids, never business
  authority.
- **Screen responsibility:** a responsibility boundary for later design work,
  not a prescribed screen inventory.

## Design readiness boundary

The map supports research questions about actors, sequence, information load,
device constraints, accessibility, error recovery and freshness. It does not
choose a visual system, navigation pattern, framework, stored location policy,
offline engine, notification provider or production screen set. Those remain
open until the relevant research, Design and Product Acceptance evidence exists.
