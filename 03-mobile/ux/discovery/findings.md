# Mobile Needfinding findings

The final AV1 Needfinding campaign is COMPLETE at **9/9 interviews**, with
three interviews per canonical segment. Evidence is de-identified and records
problem/task conditions; it does not validate a solution, prototype, Product
Acceptance or production readiness.

| Segment | Evidence state | Durable finding boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse & Dispatch Operations | 3/3 complete | Work continuity, identification, receiving, dispatch preparation and exception evidence are recurring problem/task concerns. |
| Driver Delivery Execution | 3/3 complete | Assignment, delivery attempt, incident/outcome and attributable evidence are recurring problem/task concerns. |
| B2B Buyers | 3/3 complete | Delivery attention, handoff verification, receipt and discrepancy separation are recurring problem/task concerns. |

The three solution hypotheses remain open: H1 Warehouse & Dispatch continuity,
H2 Driver Delivery Attempt and evidence, and H3 Buyer receipt and discrepancy.
The synthetic personas, As-Is task matrix and journeys are navigational research
artifacts; they contain no participant PII.
