# Mobile Needfinding findings

The final AV1 Needfinding campaign is COMPLETE at **9/9 interviews**, with
`n=3` per canonical segment. Evidence is de-identified and records
problem/task conditions; it does not validate a solution, prototype, Product
Acceptance or production readiness. Counts below describe this sample only;
they are not market-wide generalizations.

| Segment | Evidence state | Durable finding boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse & Dispatch Operations | 3/3 complete (`n=3`) | 3/3 verify information before continuing; 3/3 use an enterprise/system source; 3/3 use WhatsApp or direct communication operationally; 3/3 handle exceptions/incidents; 2/3 explicitly control lot/expiry; 2/3 report fragmentation or multiple sources; 2/3 report physical/environment conditions affecting tool use. Handoff evidence is heterogeneous: explicit evidence appears in 2/3, with no homogeneous mechanism. |
| Driver Delivery Execution | 3/3 complete (`n=3`) | 3/3 verify before delivery; 3/3 communicate incidents; 3/3 preserve an evidence mechanism; 3/3 work under adverse/mobile conditions; 3/3 use navigation/location information; 3/3 escalate or coordinate exceptions. |
| B2B Buyers | 3/3 complete (`n=3`) | 3/3 identify supply continuity; 3/3 identify punctuality; 3/3 rely on human contact for exceptions; 3/3 encounter delivery incidents/exceptions; 2/3 track or follow up; 2/3 require evidence/traceability; detailed physical receipt appears in 1/3. |

The three solution hypotheses remain open: H1 Warehouse & Dispatch continuity,
H2 Driver Delivery Attempt and evidence, and H3 Buyer receipt and discrepancy.
The synthetic personas, As-Is task matrix and journeys are navigational research
artifacts; they contain no participant PII.
