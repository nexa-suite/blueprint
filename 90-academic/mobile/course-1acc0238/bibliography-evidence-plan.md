# Bibliography and source evidence plan

The final report must use APA 7 and cite sources explicitly in the body. This
register tracks evidence without inventing papers or citations.

## Required categories

| Category | Purpose | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Business domain | Problem, sector, target segments and market relevance | Source-backed domain papers, market reports or official statistics |
| Engineering methods and techniques | Lean UX, User Stories, Impact Mapping, DDD, Scrum, architecture and validation | Cited primary/official or academic sources |
| Languages, frameworks and tools | Kotlin, Android, Flutter/Dart or KMP, REST/OpenAPI, Figma, Structurizr, Git/GitHub and selected services | Official documentation and versioned project evidence |

## Mandatory recent academic papers

At least four Scopus/Web of Science Q1/Q2 papers, no more than two years old:

| Slot | Topic | Candidate source | Verified citation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DOMAIN-01 | Domain/problem | Team must provide source | NOT PROVIDED | RESEARCH_PENDING |
| DOMAIN-02 | Domain/problem | Team must provide source | NOT PROVIDED | RESEARCH_PENDING |
| MOBILE-01 | Mobile technique/technology used | Team must provide source | NOT PROVIDED | RESEARCH_PENDING |
| MOBILE-02 | Mobile technique/technology used | Team must provide source | NOT PROVIDED | RESEARCH_PENDING |

Do not use a paper merely to fill a slot. Record DOI/URL, journal, publication
year, Scopus/WoS indexing, quartile evidence, relevance and APA 7 entry. Cite
each source in the report body.

## Source quality checklist

- Domain claim has direct source and date.
- Interview result has participant-backed evidence, not repository inference.
- Technology decision cites official documentation plus project PoC evidence.
- Historical decision preserves the date/state then and current state now.
- No source is copied into Blueprint beyond permitted publication rights.
- English is default documentation language; Spanish explanations may support
  the academic report where appropriate.
