## Durable historical themes — RESEARCH_EVIDENCE

The report provides historical signals that may seed current hypotheses:

- Commercial coordination was fragmented across WhatsApp, calls, Excel, ERP,
  email and task tools, with incomplete requests and manual transcription or
  revalidation.
- Stock visibility and reconciliation required manual checks between systems,
  warehouse staff and supervisors.
- Warehouse and Sales/operations coordination had double-entry and handoff
  friction.
- Lot, expiry, FEFO and cold-chain documentation were operational concerns,
  including cold-room constraints and temperature evidence.
- Delivery visibility, proof of delivery, receipt issues and Buyer
  communication had follow-up and exception-resolution friction.
- Operational traceability and document association were important where lots,
  expiry, temperature and delivery facts needed to be reconstructed.

These themes do not contain current measured magnitude. They may inform the
[Mobile research boundaries](../../03-mobile/product/status.md), the proposed
[value model](../../01-shared/product/economics/value-and-outcome-model.md) and
future needfinding, but they do not automatically become requirements,
personas or Product Acceptance.
